B1 Environment
The Importance of Recycling
أهمية إعادة التدوير
Recycling is one of the most important things we can do for the environment. Every year, millions of tonnes of waste are sent to landfill sites, where they cause serious damage to the land and water around them. When we recycle materials like paper, plastic, and glass, we reduce the amount of waste that goes to these sites. This not only helps the environment but also saves energy. For example, making a new bottle from recycled glass uses around thirty percent less energy than making it from new materials. Many cities now have recycling programmes, and residents are encouraged to separate their rubbish into different bins. However, experts say that recycling alone is not enough. We also need to reduce the amount we consume and reuse items whenever possible.

B1 Education
Studying Abroad
الدراسة في الخارج
More and more students today choose to study abroad for part of their education. This experience offers many benefits beyond academic knowledge. Living in a different country forces students to become more independent, as they must manage their own finances, cook their own meals, and solve problems without relying on their families. It also improves language skills dramatically, since students are surrounded by the language every day rather than only studying it in a classroom. Furthermore, studying abroad exposes young people to new cultures and ways of thinking, which can broaden their perspective on the world. Of course, the experience is not without challenges. Homesickness, cultural misunderstandings, and financial pressure are common difficulties that students face. Despite these obstacles, most students who study abroad describe it as one of the most valuable experiences of their lives.

B1 Technology
How Smartphones Changed Our Lives
كيف غيرت الهواتف الذكية حياتنا
It is hard to imagine daily life without a smartphone. In just over a decade, these devices have transformed the way we communicate, work, and entertain ourselves. Instead of carrying separate devices for calling, taking photos, or listening to music, people now rely on a single device that fits in their pocket. Smartphones have also changed how we access information; instead of searching through books or asking someone directly, we simply type a question into a search engine. This constant connectivity, however, comes with drawbacks. Many people report feeling anxious when they are separated from their phones, a condition sometimes called nomophobia. Additionally, excessive screen time has been linked to poor sleep and reduced face-to-face social interaction. Despite these concerns, it seems unlikely that smartphones will become less important in our lives anytime soon.

B1 Sports & Health
The Benefits of Regular Exercise
فوائد ممارسة الرياضة بانتظام
Doctors and health experts consistently recommend that adults engage in regular physical activity, and the reasons are well supported by research. Exercise strengthens the heart and lungs, improves blood circulation, and helps maintain a healthy body weight. Beyond the physical benefits, regular exercise has a powerful effect on mental health. Studies show that even a thirty-minute walk can reduce symptoms of stress and anxiety by releasing chemicals in the brain called endorphins. Exercise also improves sleep quality and boosts energy levels throughout the day. Unfortunately, many people struggle to maintain a consistent routine due to busy schedules or lack of motivation. Experts suggest starting with small, achievable goals, such as a ten-minute walk each day, and gradually increasing the intensity and duration over time.

B2 Technology & Society
The Rise of Remote Work
صعود العمل عن بُعد
The shift towards remote work has fundamentally changed the way millions of people approach their professional lives. What began as a temporary response to a global health crisis has, for many organisations, become a permanent feature of the modern workplace. Proponents argue that working from home increases productivity, reduces commuting time, and gives employees greater flexibility to manage their personal lives. However, critics point to several drawbacks. Social isolation can negatively affect mental health, particularly for those who live alone. Furthermore, the boundaries between work and personal life often become blurred, leading to longer working hours and increased stress. Companies, too, face challenges: maintaining a strong team culture and ensuring effective collaboration are harder when staff members rarely meet in person. The debate is unlikely to be resolved anytime soon, as both employers and employees continue to weigh the benefits against the costs.

B2 Social Media
The Double-Edged Sword of Social Media
السيف ذو الحدين: وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي
Social media platforms have fundamentally reshaped how people communicate, share information, and perceive the world around them. On one hand, these platforms have democratised access to information, allowing ordinary individuals to broadcast their opinions to a global audience and enabling social movements to organise with unprecedented speed. Small businesses, too, have benefited enormously, using platforms to reach customers without the marketing budgets that were once required. On the other hand, critics argue that social media has contributed to a rise in misinformation, as content can spread rapidly regardless of its accuracy. There are also growing concerns about the impact on mental wellbeing, particularly among younger users, who may compare their lives unfavourably to the curated, idealised versions of reality presented online. Researchers continue to debate whether the benefits of connectivity outweigh these psychological and societal costs, and it is likely that the conversation will continue as these platforms keep evolving.

B2 Environment
Understanding Climate Change
فهم التغير المناخي
Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, largely driven by human activities since the industrial era, particularly the burning of fossil fuels. This process releases greenhouse gases that trap heat in the atmosphere, leading to a gradual rise in global temperatures. The consequences are already visible: more frequent and intense heatwaves, rising sea levels, and disruptions to ecosystems that many species depend on for survival. Scientists warn that without significant reductions in emissions, these effects will worsen considerably over the coming decades. Addressing the issue requires coordinated action on multiple fronts, including transitioning to renewable energy sources, improving energy efficiency, and protecting forests, which absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. While international agreements have set ambitious targets, progress varies widely between countries, and many experts argue that current efforts remain insufficient to prevent the most severe outcomes.
